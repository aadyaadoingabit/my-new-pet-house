# 🏠 My Pet House

A cozy self-improvement app where **you are the pet**. Take care of yourself and your pet stats (Habits, Body, Mind, Heart) go up, you earn XP and you level up.

## Rooms

| Room | What it's for |
|---|---|
| 🚪 Front Door | Your pet face, level, today's care meter and a daily note |
| 🛋️ Living Room | Daily habit checklist with streaks 🔥 |
| 📚 Study | Focus timer, skill hours tracker and flashcards |
| 🏋️ Gym & Kitchen | Workouts, water, sleep and meals |
| 🌿 Calm Garden | Mood check-ins, box breathing, grounding, thought reframing (CBT) and gratitude |
| 🛏️ Bedroom | Daily journal with prompts |
| 📈 Attic | Progress charts, backups and settings |

## How to use it

Open `index.html` in your browser. That's it: no install needed.

Your data is saved in your browser only. Use **Attic → Download backup** now and then so you don't lose it.

> The Calm Garden is for everyday self-care, not a replacement for a therapist. If you're struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or a helpline (find one at [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com)).
