Welcome to your Pet House

In this house, you are the pet. Your needs go down over time, just like in real life. Fill them back up by doing real things: eating, sleeping, moving, studying, and talking to people.

  • 👆 Tap the floor to walk, tap furniture to use it
  • 🖱️ Drag to turn the camera, scroll or pinch to zoom
  • ⌨️ Or walk with WASD / arrow keys
  • 🤖 Every room has a cute AI helper. Tap one to chat.
  • 🏃 Hold Shift to run on a keyboard
  • 🪙 Earn coins for real-life self-care and decorate your house
